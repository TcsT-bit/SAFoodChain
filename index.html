1. The Problem in South Africa

It’s a national disaster: Since Sept 2024 over 890 food-borne illness incidents were reported, including child deaths from unregulated spaza shops.

Fake & Counterfeit Food

  • Fake factories: Police found hubs in Gauteng making fake baked beans, cornflakes, milk, cool drinks, even cough syrup.
  • Expired repackaging: Dates changed, goods stored next to pesticides in KZN and Gauteng.
  • Toxic chemicals: Industrial dyes in fake olive oil, pesticides like terbufos in snacks, methanol poisoning.
  • Cost: Illicit goods cost SA ~R100 billion/year and drain tax money for schools and health.

Ultra-Processed Food Overload

  • Low-income adults get ~40% of calories from ultra-processed products.
  • 18-29 year olds: over 40-60% of daily calories.
  • Linked to obesity, diabetes, heart disease, early death. SA has one of the highest obesity rates globally.

2. What You Can Do Right Now

30-Second Check Before You Buy

  • Label: Can you pronounce the first 5 ingredients? If not, put it back.
  • Price: If it’s too cheap for the brand, it’s probably fake.
  • Date: Check expiry. If it looks tampered, walk away.
  • Shop: Only buy from shops with a municipal permit displayed.

3. Grow Your Own: Food Security Starts at Home

Even a small garden cuts your risk. You control what goes into your food, and it’s cheaper than UPF snacks.

Start Small - 1m² Garden

  • Easy crops: Spinach, spring onion, tomatoes, herbs, sweet potato leaves. They grow fast in SA climate.
  • No space? Use buckets, old tyres, plastic bottles. Container gardening works.
  • Cost: R20 for seeds vs R50/week on snacks. One plant feeds you for months.

3 Steps to Start This Weekend

  1. Get old containers and poke holes at the bottom for drainage.
  2. Fill with soil + compost. Ask a neighbor or use kitchen scraps.
  3. Plant spinach or spring onion. Water every 2 days. Harvest in 3-4 weeks.

4. FoodTrack SA - The Transparency App

Idea: An app where society can see what’s happening with food markets, suppliers, distribution, and registered businesses. Transparency kills fake food.

Core Features

For Citizens

  • QR Scanner: Scan any product to see supplier, batch, expiry, registration status.
  • Spaza Shop Map: Green = compliant, Red = reported/closed.
  • Report Button: Snap photo + location, sends to NCC and ward committee.
  • Public Alerts: Get notified of fake batches and busts near you.

For Government & Inspectors

  • Inspector Dashboard: See all reports, assign cases, track resolution.
  • Business Registry: Upload/verify spaza permits and inspection results.
  • Supply Chain Log: Track batches from importer to shelf.

For Businesses

  • Business Portal: Register, upload permits, get product QR codes.
  • Compliance Score: Public score boosts customer trust.
  • Market Access: Only registered shops get listed in-app.

How It Works

Data Flow:

Manufacturer registers batch → Gets QR code printed → Shop buys batch, scans to log receipt → Consumer scans at point of sale → If mismatch/expired → Flag + Alert
